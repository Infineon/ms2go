Connect the TLI493D and TLE49012 board over USB; the page talks to it at 115200 baud. After connecting it takes 100 readings as the resting offsets – leave the board unloaded until “Calibration complete” shows. Configuration: ±17.4 mT range, 10-bit output, 800 µs ADC conversion time.
